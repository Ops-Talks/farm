# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Three overlapping user groups, each engaging different surfaces:

- **Platform / DevOps Engineers** (primary) — managing services, pipelines, environments, and infrastructure day-to-day. They live in the deployment, IaC, operators, and alerting sections.
- **Developers** — consuming the software catalog, documentation, self-service templates, and environment requests. They need fast access to what they need and no friction.
- **Engineering Leadership** — tracking scorecards, SLOs, incidents, cost analytics, and compliance. They need at-a-glance summaries and drill-down when something is wrong.

## Product Purpose

Farm is an open-source full-stack developer portal providing a centralized hub for managing software components, technical documentation, and team infrastructure. It replaces fragmented tooling with a single, consistent interface — reducing cognitive overhead and letting teams focus on building.

Success looks like: an engineer can discover any service, deploy any change, view any pipeline, and understand any incident from one place, without context-switching.

## Brand Personality

Professional, Capable, Precise — no gimmicks, no decoration without purpose. Farm communicates competence through clarity: exact spacing, deliberate color, predictable interactions. It feels like a tool built by engineers for engineers — confident enough to be quiet.

## Anti-references

- Not like Backstage — less cluttered, less sprawling, fewer walls of text.
- Not the generic pastel SaaS dashboard template — no decorative cards, no gradient hero metrics, no eyebrow labels on every section.
- Not enterprise-corporate — no heavy chrome, no busy toolbars, no visual debt.

## Design Principles

1. **Purposeful minimalism** — every element earns its place. If it doesn't inform, enable, or orient, remove it.
2. **Clarity over density** — information-dense surfaces must still be scannable. Hierarchy, not clutter.
3. **Developer confidence** — precise positioning, consistent component language, predictable interactions. Nothing wobbles, nothing surprises.
4. **Systematic rhythm** — spacing, typography, and color follow a coherent scale, not ad-hoc decisions. The system is the authority.
5. **Identity through restraint** — the brand lives in its purple primary, tight tracking, and clean layout. Not in decorative flourishes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard web accessibility as provided by the platform (Next.js, shadcn/ui, Tailwind). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Maintain ≥4.5:1 body text contrast. All interactive elements must be keyboard-reachable and have visible focus indicators.
