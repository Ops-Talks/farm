Farm Observability stack deployed successfully.

Components installed:
  - kube-prometheus-stack (Prometheus, Grafana, Alertmanager)
  - Loki (log aggregation)
  - Tempo (distributed tracing)
  - Alloy (telemetry collector)
  - Pyroscope (continuous profiling)

Access Grafana:
  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ .Release.Name }}-grafana 3000:80 -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}
  Open http://localhost:3000

For full configuration reference see:
  https://github.com/Ops-Talks/farm/tree/main/deploy/helm/observability
